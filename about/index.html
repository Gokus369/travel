About us

A travel company on the Kerala coast

Wave Story Ventures is based in Varkala, on the red cliffs above the Arabian Sea. From here, we plan journeys across the whole of India: tour packages and travel guides, all in one place.

Our name comes from a simple idea. Waves arrive one after another, and each one is a little different. So are good trips. The best ones leave you with something to tell, and that is what we plan for: not just a list of sights, but a story you will want to share when you get home.

Whether it is a slow week on our own coast or a high road through Ladakh, you will deal with the same people from your first message to your last day.

A person, not a portal

You talk to someone who reads your message and plans your trip, from the first idea to the journey home.

Honest about the seasons

If a place is wrong for your month, we will say so and suggest somewhere that is right.

Your trip, your way

Our packages are starting points. Hotels, pace, and route can all change to suit you.
