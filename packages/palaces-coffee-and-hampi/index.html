Palaces, Coffee and Hampi

Karnataka in one sweep: a royal city, a coffee estate and a lost capital.

Mysuru → Coorg → Hampi

Duration
7 nights, 8 days
Best months
Oct–Mar
Pace

Good for

Get a quote for this trip

Karnataka rewards the traveller who keeps moving. Mysuru is all silk, sandalwood and palace light. Coorg is cool and green, with coffee growing under tall shade trees. And Hampi, once the capital of the Vijayanagara empire, is unlike anywhere else in India: temple ruins scattered across a landscape of giant granite boulders.

The highlights

  • Mysore Palace and the view from Chamundi Hill
  • The Tibetan Golden Temple at Bylakuppe
  • A walk through a working Coorg coffee estate
  • The stone chariot at Vittala Temple in Hampi
  • A coracle on the Tungabhadra and sunset from Hemakuta Hill

The story, day by day

  1. Day 1

    Bengaluru to Mysuru

    About three and a half hours by road, stopping at the island fort of Srirangapatna on the way.

  2. Day 2

    Royal Mysuru

    Tour Mysore Palace, go up Chamundi Hill, and wander Devaraja Market for flowers, silk and sandalwood.

  3. Day 3

    Into Coorg

    About three hours west, with a stop at the Tibetan settlement and Golden Temple at Bylakuppe, then check in among the coffee.

  4. Day 4

    A coffee day

    Walk an estate, see Abbey Falls, and end the afternoon at Raja's Seat as the hills turn blue.

  5. Day 5

    Overnight towards Hampi

    Drive back to Bengaluru, about six hours, for the overnight train to Hosapete.

  6. Day 6

    Hampi's sacred centre

    Arrive in the morning. See Virupaksha Temple, climb Hemakuta Hill, and visit the Vittala Temple and its stone chariot.

  7. Day 7

    Hampi's royal centre

    The Royal Enclosure, Lotus Mahal and Elephant Stables, then a coracle on the Tungabhadra and a last sunset among the boulders.

  8. Day 8

    Departure

    Onward from Hosapete by train, or by road to Vidyanagar or Hubballi airport.
