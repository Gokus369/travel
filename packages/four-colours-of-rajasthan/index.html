Four Colours of Rajasthan

Pink Jaipur, blue Jodhpur, gold Jaisalmer, white Udaipur.

Jaipur → Pushkar → Jodhpur → Jaisalmer → Ranakpur → Udaipur

Duration
9 nights, 10 days
Best months
Oct–Mar
Pace

Rajasthan is best told in colours. You start in the rose-coloured bazaars of Jaipur, look down on the blue houses of Jodhpur from one of India's mightiest forts, reach the honey-gold citadel of Jaisalmer at the edge of the Thar, and finish in Udaipur, where white palaces rise straight from the lake.

The highlights

  • Amber Fort and the bazaars of Jaipur
  • Mehrangarh Fort above the blue city of Jodhpur
  • A night at a desert camp near the Sam dunes
  • The living fort and havelis of Jaisalmer
  • The marble Jain temple at Ranakpur
  • An evening boat on Lake Pichola

The story, day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Jaipur

    Pick-up and an easy evening in the old city.

  2. Day 2

    The Pink City

    Amber Fort, the City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the Hawa Mahal, with time for block prints and jewellery in the bazaars.

  3. Day 3

    Pushkar to Jodhpur

    A stop at the sacred lake of Pushkar on the way to Jodhpur, around six to seven hours on the road.

  4. Day 4

    The Blue City

    Mehrangarh Fort, the marble Jaswant Thada, and a walk through the blue lanes around the clock tower.

  5. Day 5

    To Jaisalmer

    About five hours west as the land turns to desert. See the sunset from Gadisar Lake or the fort walls.

  6. Day 6

    Fort and dunes

    Walk the living fort and the carved Patwon ki Haveli, then head to the Sam dunes for sunset and a night at a desert camp.

  7. Day 7

    Back across the desert

    Return to Jodhpur, about five hours, with the evening free. It breaks up the long road to Udaipur.

  8. Day 8

    Ranakpur to Udaipur

    See the marble halls of the Ranakpur Jain temple on the way to Udaipur, around six hours in all.

  9. Day 9

    The city of lakes

    Tour the City Palace and Jagdish Temple, then take a boat on Lake Pichola at sunset.

  10. Day 10

    Departure

    Transfer to Udaipur airport or station.
