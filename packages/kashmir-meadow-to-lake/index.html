The Himalaya

Kashmir, Meadow to Lake

Shikaras on Dal Lake, the Gulmarg gondola and the river valleys of Pahalgam.

Srinagar → Gulmarg → Pahalgam → Sonamarg

Duration
6 nights, 7 days
Best months
Apr–Oct
Pace

Good for

Get a quote for this trip

Few places in India are as easy on the eye as the Kashmir Valley. Start with a night on a cedar houseboat in Srinagar, ride the gondola into the high meadows at Gulmarg, follow the Lidder River through Pahalgam, and end at the glacier valley of Sonamarg. Distances are short and the days are gentle, which makes this a favourite for couples and families.

The highlights

  • A night on a Dal Lake houseboat
  • A shikara ride at sunset
  • The Mughal gardens of Nishat and Shalimar
  • The Gulmarg gondola
  • Betaab and Aru valleys near Pahalgam

The story, day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Srinagar

    Pick-up from the airport, check in to a houseboat, and take a shikara across Dal Lake at sunset.

  2. Day 2

    Gardens and old Srinagar

    See Nishat and Shalimar Baghs, then Jamia Masjid and the old city's wooden shrines and craft shops.

  3. Day 3

    Gulmarg

    About two hours to Gulmarg for the gondola, high meadows in summer and snow in the colder months.

  4. Day 4

    To Pahalgam

    About four and a half hours by road, passing the saffron fields of Pampore. Evening by the Lidder River.

  5. Day 5

    Pahalgam valleys

    A day out to Betaab and Aru valleys, with pony rides or gentle walks.

  6. Day 6

    Sonamarg

    About four hours along the Sindh River to Sonamarg and the Thajiwas glacier valley, then on to Srinagar for the night.

  7. Day 7

    Departure

    Transfer to Srinagar airport. Allow extra time for security checks.
